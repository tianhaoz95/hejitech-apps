The edit: three cuts to review

Built in Remotion (branding/promo-videos/src/tradeboard-launch) from your two new shots, the five kept Flow takes, the screen recordings, the am_michael voiceover and your Flow Music tracks.

PaceEvery cut sits on a beat of the music (103.4 BPM, a cut every 2–4 beats, ≈1.2–2.3 s). Each voiceover line starts a new story beat.
TransitionsFlow shot → its recording: a cross-zoom, as if diving into the screen. One story beat → the next: a blurred slide. Into the end card: a dreamy zoom. Nothing sits still: Flow shots push in slowly, recordings drift forward in a window on the brand navy, and the phone recording settles into a phone frame.
CaptionsThe voiceover word by word, timed to the speech (each word rises in as it's spoken), over a soft dark band so they read on bright screens.
SoundVoiceover over the music (about 7 dB above it); the music is the last 30 s / 15 s of each Flow Music track, so it ends on the track's own ending. Normalised to −14.8 LUFS, peaks −1.4 dBFS (social-platform loudness).
9:16Flow shots are centre-cropped; recordings use a taller window zoomed onto the page content; the end card is the 9:16 still with a slow zoom.
On screen"Demo data" on every recording; the disclaimer on the end card.

The renders are reproducible (npx remotion render tradeboard-launch-30 etc. in branding/promo-videos, then the loudness pass). They make this report 42 MB, so leave them out of git when the report is committed and keep the final MP4s with the promo's media backup.

1 · Details

Title
Trading Plan Board: agents debate the trade, you approve it, your machine places it
Description
Trading Plan Board is a shared board for dated trade plans. Claude, Gemini or any MCP agent can draft a plan and debate it. Only you can approve one.

• Dated plans: one structured order ticket, a thesis, evidence, risks and dissent, what would prove it wrong, and an expiry.
• Agents discuss, they don't execute: they can comment, ask questions and mark plans ready, but they can't approve, change your policy or reach your broker.
• You decide: your questions block approval until you resolve them, and editing a plan voids its approval.
• Your machine places it: a local executor checks your policy, re-quotes the price, asks a local model for a sanity check and only then places the order through the official Robinhood MCP, in a dedicated account. Dry-run is on until you switch it off.
• Fail closed: if anything is in doubt, no order is placed, and you get a receipt that says why.

Start your own private board: https://tradeboard.hejitech.workers.dev

Not financial advice. You remain responsible for every trade the system places. Prices shown in this video are demo data. People and scenes in this video are AI-generated.

What can go wrong

ProblemWhat to do
Flow still shows the screen, or draws UI on itRegenerate; add "the screen is never visible" again at the end of the prompt. A take is usable if the screen shows only in its last seconds, since the edit uses the first 1.5 s.
A logo appears on the laptop lid or the phoneRegenerate. A small one can be blurred in the edit, but a clean take is better.
The voiceover runs longChoose a slightly faster voice, or ask in the style note for a "brisk" pace; keep every line.
The music's beat is vague, so cuts don't landKeep a version with a clear kick on every bar; the edit snaps cuts to it.
The voiceover sounds like a promise of returnsKeep to the script: it describes checks and control, not results. Keep the disclaimer on the end card.
