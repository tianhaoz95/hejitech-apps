The story: the only person underground who isn't waiting

A morning metro carriage, packed and sleepy. The train dives into the tunnel, the lights flicker, and every phone in the carriage stalls at once: people shake them, tilt them, hold them up. Sam, in his everyday-looking glasses and earbuds, keeps reading. Bea, standing next to him, holds her phone up to the ceiling on tiptoe and asks the carriage: "Is anyone's news loading?" Sam turns to the camera: "Mine was ready last night." He glances to the corner of his view: today's edition is there. A swipe of his thumb across the ring moves card to card; a hold, and the deep dive plays in his earbuds while Bea presses her phone flat against the window. Flashback to the night before: Sam types his brief on his laptop, closes it and switches off the lamp; his agent does the rest while he sleeps. Back on the train, it bursts out of the tunnel into daylight, every phone in the carriage buzzes at once, and Bea beams: "It loaded!" The doors open. "Oh, this is my stop." Sam, already done, gives the camera a nod and steps off.

What can go wrong

ProblemWhat to do
Flow draws a real city's metro: line colours, maps, station names, a transit logoRegenerate with "no signs, no maps, no station names" again at the end, or use a take where it's out of focus or cropped.
A phone or the laptop turns toward the camera and shows a screenUse the part before it does, or regenerate; every prompt says the screens face away.
Sam's glasses come out bulky, like goggles, or change between shotsAlways add Sam; pick the portrait whose glasses look most like ordinary glasses; regenerate with "ordinary everyday glasses, thin frames".
Sam and Bea blend into each other in shots 1, 2, 5, 7Make shot 2 first as the test. If Flow keeps mixing them, make Bea's part as its own shot and the edit cuts between them.
Bea comes across as annoying or mockedKeep takes where she's hopeful and funny, not cross; her last line gets the laugh, so she wins the ending too.
The tunnel looks too dark for facesThe prompts keep the carriage lit; if a take is murky, regenerate with "the carriage brightly lit, only the windows dark".
Flow ad-libs extra linesThe edit keeps only the written lines and mutes the rest; I transcribe every take first.
