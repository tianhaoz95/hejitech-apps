Everyone in the city runs for the train. She doesn't.

The plan for Headway's promo: a 30-second comedy plus 15-second cuts in 16:9 and 9:16, set in today's San Francisco and on the Peninsula. Every morning the city sprints for Caltrain. Lin strolls, coffee in hand, and still makes every train, because her next train is in her glasses and her phone taps her when it's time. Her running gag is Max, a commuter who sprints for everything and makes nothing. The style comes from your reference: a photoreal, expressive lead who plays to the camera, in warm, glowing cinematic light. You make the characters, the shots and the music in Google Flow from the prompts below; I make the screen recordings of the glasses, iPhone and Watch apps, the voiceover and the edit.

The story: the calmest commuter in the city

A Bay Area morning. The whole city is running: scooters, laptops under arms, toast in mouths, all heading for the train. Lin walks the other way through the crowd, sipping her coffee, and narrates to camera like the star of her own show. Max sprints past her and dives at closing doors, and misses. She doesn't run, because Headway shows her next train in her glasses, the ring flips through the ones after it, and her phone taps her when it's time to go and again before her stop. In the payoff she steps off at her stop. Max dozed off, missed it, and is carried away pressed against the window, while she waves her croissant at him.

Your next Caltrain, at a glance.

Lin · 3 · Character info ("Describe how your character acts…")
Lin is the calmest person in a city that is always running late. She never hurries: she strolls, sips her coffee and takes her time, because she already knows when her train leaves. Unbothered, dry and a little smug, with a playful sense of humour.

How she acts on camera: she plays to the camera like the star of her own show, with knowing glances, a slow raised eyebrow, a small satisfied smile and the occasional wink.
