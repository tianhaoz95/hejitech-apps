App Review notes · corrected here; paste after adding your contact
No sign-in is needed: tap "Use without an account" on the first screen to try every logging feature (voice, tap, timeline, the watch app). Sign in with Apple creates an account for household sync.

Voice: tap the microphone and say e.g. "bottle, 90 ml". Speech recognition and the decision model run on the device; no audio is uploaded from the iPhone.

The Even G2 glasses and R1 ring screens in Settings are optional companion hardware; the app works fully without them.

There are no in-app purchases. Account deletion: Settings → Delete account & data (signed in) deletes the user's Wee Hours account: the household's log and photos, memberships, invites and assistant access. With Sign in with Apple it first confirms with Apple so the token can be revoked. The same sign-in also opens our other apps, so their data isn't touched. Without an account the same place reads "Erase this phone's log".

Risks and open questions

RiskWhyWhat to do
A reviewer says deletion doesn't delete the account (5.1.1(v))By your rule, deleting removes only the app's own data and keeps the shared HEJI login. Apple usually accepts this when the app's account and data are gone and the user is signed out.The review notes now explain it. If Apple rejects, the reply is that sign-in is shared across apps by the same developer, so each app deletes its own account. Changing the behaviour is your call.
