How "Delete account & data" works now

The household goes first, as before: files, then delete_household. Then the app calls the Worker's new POST /api/account/delete with its session. The Worker revokes the user's assistant grants, revokes the Sign in with Apple token when it can, and deletes the auth user; every Wee Hours row that points at the user cascades with it. The account page on the site does the same, minus the Apple step.

A trade-off you should know about: the login is the shared HEJI account, so deleting it also ends the user's sign-in to any other HEJI app (Waypack today), and that app's rows tied to the user go with it. Both confirm texts, the app's and the account page's, say so. This was option A in the scan. If you'd rather keep the login, the alternative is to rename the button "Delete household data", which risks a 5.1.1(v) rejection.

TRACK WHAT YOU NEED
Feeds (bottle, breast, pumping, solids), diapers, sleep, growth, and optional care, health and activity entries like baths, medication, temperature and tummy time. Turn on only what you use.

YOURS

Wee Hours records what happened. It doesn't give medical advice.

Age rating answers: every content question "None", every yes/no "No", except health or wellness topics: Yes (the app logs medication, temperature and symptoms). Medical or treatment information: None, since the app gives no advice. Apple computed 4+. The answers are your attestation, so look them over in App Information → Age Rating.

Your steps, in order

Steps 1–8 can happen in any order on the same day; 9 needs 2; 11 needs all the rest.

  1. Confirm the price model. Paid with Family Sharing is what the text now says. Pick the price tier (the old site had $79.99 for lifetime; nothing in the repo decides the new one) and write it in the Billing row of projects/weehours/docs/decisions.md. That row still describes the old subscription.
  2. Look over the commit and push it. Pushing deploys the Support, Privacy, Terms and account pages and the Worker's deletion endpoint (Cloudflare Workers Builds). Wait for weehours-ci.yml to go green: cut_release.sh refuses a red or unpushed main. 
    git show --stat HEAD
git push
    The root CLAUDE.md has an uncommitted change from other work that isn't part of this commit; commit or stash it, because cut_release.sh refuses a dirty tree.
  3. Create a Sign in with Apple key and give it to the Worker, so deletion can revoke Apple tokens (Apple asks for this under 5.1.1(v)). In the developer portal, Certificates, IDs & Profiles → Keys: +, name it "Wee Hours SIWA", tick Sign in with Apple, Configure → primary App ID com.hejitech.weehours, register, and download the .p8 (it downloads only once). Then, from projects/weehours/services/web: 
    npx wrangler secret put APPLE_SIWA_KEY_ID --env production
npx wrangler secret put APPLE_SIWA_PRIVATE_KEY --env production < AuthKey_XXXXXXXXXX.p8
    Without it, deletion still deletes the login and just skips the revocation. The privacy policy now says the app asks Apple to revoke access, so do this step before launch. Store the .p8 with your other keys, not in the repo.
  4. Business (App Store Connect → Business): the Paid Apps agreement is Active, and tax forms and a bank account are filled in. A price can't be set until it is.
  5. Pricing and Availability (the app → Pricing and Availability): set the price, keep all countries or pick some, and turn on Family Sharing. It can't be turned off again for this app, which is fine for a one-time purchase.
  6. App Privacy (the app → App Privacy): enter the answers in §4 and press Publish. The API can't write this form.
  7. App Review contact (version page → App Review Information): your first and last name, phone and email. I left these empty; they're personal and Apple uses them to call. Notes are filled in, and "Sign-in required" is off.
  8. Screenshots. The version page needs 6.9" iPhone (1320 × 2868) and, because the app has a watch app, Apple Watch shots. The script now shoots at 6.9" and skips What's New. It ran, but the local test household's log is thin (one feed, an old diaper), so the shots aren't good enough for the store. Your iPhone 13 is 6.1", a size the store doesn't take, so shoot on the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator (created for this; 1320 × 2868) with a Release build signed in to your own account, so the log is real: 
    cd projects/weehours/apps/ios && xcodegen generate
xcodebuild -project WeeHours.xcodeproj -scheme App -configuration Release -destination "platform=iOS Simulator,name=iPhone 17 Pro Max" -derivedDataPath .derived build CODE_SIGN_IDENTITY=- CODE_SIGNING_REQUIRED=NO
xcrun simctl boot "iPhone 17 Pro Max"; open -a Simulator
xcrun simctl install "iPhone 17 Pro Max" .derived/Build/Products/Release-iphonesimulator/WeeHours.app
    Sign in (Google works in the simulator; Apple needs an Apple Account signed in to the simulator), then ⌘S in Simulator saves each screen. Or write in your notes "seed a realistic demo week and re-run the screenshot script", and an agent can do it on the local stack. Watch: open the watch app on a watch, then press the side button and Digital Crown together, or use xcrun simctl io <watch udid> screenshot on a Series 11/12 46 mm simulator.
  9. Cut 1.0.0, once CI is green after step 2: 
    scripts/cut_release.sh weehours 1.0.0 --dry-run   # look first
scripts/cut_release.sh weehours 1.0.0 --watch
    The TestFlight run waits for Apple's verdict and fails if the build is rejected. The release also attaches weehours-glasses-1.0.0.ehpk for Even Hub (separate, by hand).
  10. Try 1.0.0 from TestFlight on your iPhone. Sign in with Apple, log by voice and tap, check the watch, open Settings → About → Acknowledgements. Then sign in with a second, test Apple Account (not your own; deleting it also ends its Waypack sign-in) and use Delete account & data. You should see the Apple sheet, then the sign-in screen. On the web, /account/ with that account should no longer sign in to the old data.
  11. Choose the build and submit. On the 1.0.0 version page: Build → replace 202610151828 with the 1.0.0 build, upload the screenshots, check the text, then Add for Review → Submit.
  12. After approval: release it (the version is set to release automatically after approval; switch to manual first if you want to pick the day). Then put the store link on the site: VITE_APP_STORE_URL=https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823293298 in projects/weehours/services/web/.env.production, and push.
