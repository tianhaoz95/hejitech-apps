Night service on the Harbor Line becomes permanent

The Harbor Line's summer trial of all-night trains ends this week, and the service stays. From Friday 7 November, trains run through the night every night. Between 01:00 and 05:00 they run every 20 minutes with three cars instead of six.

Ridership in the trial's night hours averaged 3,100 trips a night, mostly late shifts, late shows and early flights. The last-train time of 00:40 goes away.

Night trains stop at every station. Quay Street's lifts stay on all night.
