Why most hosts fail the test

The hours were real. The paperwork wasn't.

Requirement

The IRS requires contemporaneous records — logged as the work happens, not reconstructed later from memory.

Precedent

Courts have consistently sided with taxpayers who kept real-time logs, and against those who estimated their hours after the fact.

Burden of proof

Proving material participation is entirely on the host. If it isn't written down as it happens, it doesn't hold up under review.
