    1.6 Full Description (max 4,000 characters)

    Paste as-is into the "Description" field in App Store Connect:

    Contempo helps short-term rental hosts log the hours they spend managing their properties — in a form that actually holds up if the IRS ever asks for proof.

    If you're using the STR "material participation" strategy to reclassify rental losses against your W-2 or active income, the tax code doesn't just require that you did the work — it requires contemporaneous records, logged as the work happens, not reconstructed at tax time from memory. Most hosts who fail an audit don't fail because they didn't do the hours. They fail because they can't prove it.

    Key Features on iOS & Apple Watch:

    • Hands-Free Voice & Text Capture: Speak or type what you just did — Contempo turns it into a structured, timestamped entry (property, task, duration, category), asking one quick follow-up only if it needs clarification.

    • Apple Watch Companion App: Record work-log voice memos directly from your wrist with one tap. Memos are encoded locally and synced automatically when back online.

    • Photo Evidence & Mileage Tracking: Attach photo evidence to any entry, or track drive time with automatic GPS mileage trip tracking and Live Activities.

    • Contemporaneous Risk Flagging: Entries logged long after the fact, or described too vaguely, get flagged automatically — visibly, on the entry itself — so you can fix a weak record before it becomes an issue with your CPA or the IRS.

    • Audit-Ready Progress & Exports: Track your progress toward 100-hour and 500-hour Material Participation tests on a clean dashboard, and generate audit-ready CSV, PDF, or JSON packages for your CPA.

    • On-Device Privacy: Transcription can run entirely on-device via local Whisper and LiteRT LLM models (nothing leaves your phone), or use cloud AI processing — customizable anytime in Settings.

    Contempo is built by Hejitech for real hosts — this is an early release, and features will continuously expand based on real host usage.

    8. Compliance Gaps to Close Before Submission

    Three compliance gaps must be addressed by engineering before submitting the build to App Store Review:

    Gap 1 — CRITICAL: Missing "Sign in with Apple" (Guideline 4.8) Issue: Apple App Store Review Guideline 4.8 explicitly mandates that any app offering third-party social authentication (such as Google Sign-In) MUST also offer Sign in with Apple as an equivalent option.
    Codebase State: ios/Contempo/Sources/Auth/GoogleSignInButton.swift currently implements Google Sign-In only.
    Required Fix: Implement AuthenticationServices framework's SignInWithAppleButton in the auth flow alongside Google Sign-In, and configure the Sign in with Apple capability in ios/project.yml and Apple Developer Portal.
    Gap 2 — Privacy Policy URL Hosting Issue: App Store Connect requires a live, publicly accessible Privacy Policy URL.
    Codebase State: Privacy Policy content is ready in web/src/legal/content.ts and rendered at /legal/privacy, but web/ has not yet been deployed to production Firebase Hosting.
    Required Fix: Deploy web/ via firebase deploy --only hosting and verify https://contempo-platform.web.app/legal/privacy before pasting the link into App Store Connect.
    Gap 3 — Demo Account Setup for App Review Issue: Reviewers cannot test behind Google Sign-In without active test credentials.
    Required Fix: Provision a dedicated test Google/Apple account pre-populated with sample STR properties and entries, and document credentials in Section 5 prior to pressing Submit for Review.
