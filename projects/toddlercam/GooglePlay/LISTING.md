## Full description (4000)

ToddlerCam is a playful, child-safe camera for babies, toddlers and the parents trying to photograph them.

Hand the phone to your toddler, or prop it up in front of them, and take the picture from your wrist: the Wear OS remote fires the shutter and plays silly sounds that make your little one look straight at the lens.

TODDLER-FRIENDLY CAMERA
• Big, bright shutter button and a full-screen live preview.
• Swap between the back and selfie cameras with one tap.
• Every tap on the screen pops a bubble, star, heart or balloon with a happy sound, so curious fingers are rewarded instead of leaving the app.
• Photos are saved as dated keepsakes straight to your phone's gallery (Pictures/ToddlerCam).

FACE MASKS AND SILLY SOUNDS
• Nine fun overlays: Crown, Lion, Sunglasses, Kitty, Puppy, Unicorn, Panda, Bunny and Star Eyes.
• Attention grabbers: puppy bark, duck quack, kitten meow and baby giggle, played from the phone or the watch.

TODDLER LOCK AND PARENTAL GATE
• Toddler Safe Lock turns the screen into a bubble-popping touch canvas while the camera keeps running.
• Settings and the photo gallery sit behind a simple grown-up math question.
• Parent Hub explains how to use Android App Pinning so your toddler stays inside ToddlerCam.

WEAR OS PARENT REMOTE
• Giant wrist shutter with haptic feedback.
• Silly soundboard and attention grabbers on your wrist.
• Pick a face mask, swap cameras or lock the phone screen remotely.
• Review the last photo right on your watch.
• Tile and complication for one-tap access from your watch face.

PRIVATE AND CHILD-SAFE
• No accounts, no data collection
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• No in-app purchases
• Photos stay on your phone. Phone and watch talk only to each other over your own Bluetooth connection.
