One morning, Max rode his bicycle down the lane. Ring ring went the bell.
The farmer waved from his big green tractor.
Suddenly a fire truck raced past. Nee naw, nee naw!
A little pig said oink oink, and the rooster crowed, cock a doodle doo.
Duck down, said Grandma, the branch is very low.
Max sneezed. Achoo!
Back home, the door creaked open, and a robot said beep boop.
Everyone shouted hooray!
Then Max yawned, and that was the end of the day. The end.
