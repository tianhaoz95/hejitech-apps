Deep in the jungle, a curious monkey swung from tree to tree.
He remembered the elephant he had met last summer.
Then a hungry tiger crept closer and closer!
The monkey shouted, and the tiger ran far away.
A huge dinosaur stomped through the trees.
Nobody had ever seen a dragon in this jungle.
A frog hopped onto a lily pad and croaked.
The wolf howled at the moon.
The owl was quiet and sleepy.
Then everyone fell fast asleep.
