One sunny morning, Lily walked to the farm with her grandpa.
The cow looked up from the grass and said moo.
A little puppy came running out of the barn, barking and wagging its tail.
But the horse was fast asleep in the stable.
Then the rooster flew onto the fence. Oh wait, there is no rooster on this farm.
Suddenly the sky grew dark and it started to rain.
Thunder rumbled over the hills.
Lily and grandpa ran inside and knocked on the farmhouse door.
The cat purred by the fireplace.
Soon the rain stopped and the sun came out.
The end. Good night!
