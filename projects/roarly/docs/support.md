# Roarly support

**Roarly doesn't play a sound.** Check that the lantern is lit and your words appear under it (turn on
Settings › Show my words). If a sound is offered as a card, Roarly wasn't sure: tap it. Settings ›
"How much Roarly does by itself" › Mostly automatic plays more on its own.

**A sound played that shouldn't have.** Choose "Mostly ask me" to have Roarly play only the sure ones
(like "the lion roared") and offer the rest.

**It's too loud at bedtime.** Pick Bedtime at the top of the story screen: big sounds are softer and
everything fades out when you say "the end" or "good night".

**The first story says "Getting the speech model ready".** iOS downloads its on-device speech model
once; after that Roarly works offline.

**Apple Watch.** Install Roarly on the watch from the Watch app, open it while a story is running, and
tap the suggestions there. Settings › Play sounds on › Apple Watch plays them on the watch speaker.

**Restore Roarly Pro.** Settings › Restore purchases.

Still stuck? Open an issue at https://github.com/tianhaoz95/hejitech-apps/issues.
