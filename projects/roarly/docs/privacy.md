# Roarly privacy policy

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Roarly is made by HEJI TECHNOLOGY LLC. The short version: **Roarly collects nothing.**

- **The microphone** is on only while the lantern on the story screen is lit (and, if you leave the
  story running, in the background until you stop it). What you say is turned into text **on your
  iPhone** by Apple's on-device speech recognition so Roarly can pick a sound. The text is kept in
  memory for the story and forgotten when it ends. No audio is recorded or saved.
- **Smart listening** uses Apple's on-device language model (or a small model inside the app) to
  read a sentence. That also happens entirely on your iPhone.
- **No account, no servers.** Roarly has no backend. It sends nothing to us or to anyone else, and
  contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- **Purchases** (Roarly Pro) are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
- **Your settings** (mode, your own trigger words) stay on your iPhone, and are passed to your Apple
  Watch over Apple's watch connection when you use the watch app.
- **Children.** Roarly is meant to be used by a parent telling a story. It collects no information
  from anyone, including children.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/tianhaoz95/hejitech-apps/issues or see the
[support page](/support).
