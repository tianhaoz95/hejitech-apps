Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version. G2 Digest stores your brief and the editions your own agent publishes, so your glasses can show them. We use them for nothing else. No ads, no tracking, no data sales, and no AI runs on our servers. You can delete everything at any time.

Who we are

G2 Digest is made by HEJI TECHNOLOGY LLC (“we”, “us”): this website, its API and the G2 Digest app for Even Realities G2 glasses on Even Hub. Questions about this policy go to info@hejitech.com.

What we collect

Account

  • Your email address and the identifier from Sign in with Google.
  • Your account is a HEJI account: the same sign-in works in our other apps. Each app keeps its own data, and G2 Digest never shares your brief or editions with them.

Your brief and editions

  • Your brief: its sections, sources, instructions, glossary, delivery time and time zone.
  • Each edition your agent publishes: the full text with its sources, the cards, and the audio files your agent uploads.

Only you can read your brief and editions. The database enforces this per account.

Glasses, feeds and agents

  • Paired glasses and podcast feeds: a label, when they were paired and when they were last used.
  • Agent tokens and connected assistants: a name, when they were created and last used.
  • Tokens and pairing codes are stored only as hashes. A pairing code expires after ten minutes.
  • To stop code guessing, we count wrong pairing codes per network address for ten minutes, then forget them.

The glasses app

The G2 Digest app runs inside the Even Realities app on your phone. It uses one permission, network, only to fetch your editions from G2 Digest (g2digest.hejitech.workers.dev) and your edition's audio from our storage provider, Supabase (a supabase.co address). It keeps its pairing token, the last edition, your place in it and your audio setting in the Even app's storage on your phone, and saves audio files there for offline listening. It doesn't use the microphone, camera, location or photos. What the Even Realities app itself collects is covered by Even Realities' privacy policy.

Agents you connect

An edition is written by an agent you choose and connect (for example Claude, with a G2 Digest sign-in or an agent token). That agent reads your brief and publishes editions to your account. It does its research and writing with its own provider, whose privacy policy covers what it does with your brief. G2 Digest runs no AI and makes no audio on its servers. Disconnect assistants and revoke tokens at any time on the Pair page.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal data, or share it for advertising.
  • There are no advertising or analytics tools, here or in the glasses app.
  • This website uses no advertising or analytics cookies. When you sign in, it keeps your session in your browser's local storage.
  • We never use your brief, editions or audio to train models.

Service providers

These providers run G2 Digest for us and process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase stores accounts, briefs, editions and audio.
  • Cloudflare serves this website, the API the glasses use, and the agent (MCP) connection.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google.

How long we keep it

We keep your brief until you delete it, and your 10 newest editions: when an 11th arrives, the oldest edition and its audio are deleted. Pairing codes expire after ten minutes, and wrong-code counts after ten minutes.

Your choices and rights

  • Access: every edition, with its sources, is on the Edition page, and your brief on the Brief page.
  • Correct: edit your brief at any time. Your agent's next edition follows it.
  • Unpair: revoke glasses and feeds on the Pair page. They stop working right away.
  • Delete: Delete all my G2 Digest data on the Pair page permanently deletes your brief, editions, audio, paired glasses, feeds, tokens and connected assistants. To delete your sign-in as well, email info@hejitech.com from the address on your account.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have more rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to a data protection authority. Email us and we'll help.

Children

G2 Digest isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Tokens are stored only as hashes, and audio is served through links that expire.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on this site before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
