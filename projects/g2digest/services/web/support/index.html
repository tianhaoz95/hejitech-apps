Getting started

How do I set it up?
  1. Install G2 Digest from Even Hub in the Even Realities app.
  2. Sign in on this site and write your brief: the sections you want, their sources and what counts.
  3. Open Pair, get a 6-digit code, and type it into G2 Digest in the Even app.
  4. Connect the agent that builds your editions (Agents) and run it on your delivery days.
The glasses show a tour instead of my edition

The tour plays whenever there's nothing to read yet. Its last card says why: the glasses aren't paired, your agent hasn't published an edition, or the phone was offline before anything was fetched. Once an edition is published, the glasses show it the next time G2 Digest opens, or within fifteen minutes while it's open.

The pairing code doesn't work

A code works once and expires after ten minutes: get a new one on the Pair page. After ten wrong codes, pairing pauses for ten minutes.

On the glasses

Which gestures does the R1 ring use?
  • Swipe down / up: next or previous card.
  • Tap: play or pause the card's short audio (the glance).
  • Tap, then hold: play the card's deep dive. The same actions are also in the card's menu.
  • Double-tap: stop the audio. With nothing playing, it asks whether to exit.
Audio stops when my phone locks

In G2 Digest on your phone, set Audio to In my podcast app, then add your private feed from the Pair page to your podcast app. The glasses then show which episode to play.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app keeps the last edition and saves its audio on your phone, so a commute without signal still works.
