How it works

  1. Write your brief: sections, sources, what counts, what a quiet day looks like. It's yours to edit, and the agent never changes it.
  2. An agent builds the edition before your commute: the full text with sources, the cards, and the audio, checked against the card rules.
  3. Pair the glasses once with a 6-digit code. The plugin fetches each edition and keeps it for offline.
  4. On the train: swipe for the next card, tap for a short glance, tap then hold for the deep dive, double-tap to stop (or, with nothing playing, back to your list of editions).

Made for a hostile moment

A moving train, no free hands and a real risk of motion sickness from reading. So a card is one screen with one number and a two-second decision, and everything deeper is audio.
