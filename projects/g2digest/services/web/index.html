How it works

The hard work happens before you leave.

On the train the plugin only plays back an edition it already has. Everything slow happens the night before.

  1. 01

    Write your brief

    Sections, sources, what counts and what a quiet day looks like. It's yours to edit; the agent never changes it.

  2. 02

    Your agent builds the edition

    Before your commute it writes the full text with sources, the cards and the audio, and checks them against the card rules.

  3. 03

    Pair the glasses once

    Type a 6-digit code into G2 Digest in the Even app. The plugin fetches each edition and keeps it for offline.

  4. 04

    Glance on the train

    Swipe for the next card, tap for a short glance, tap then hold for the deep dive, double-tap to stop.

Made for a hostile moment

A moving train, no free hands, no signal.

Reading on the move invites motion sickness, and underground nothing loads. So every part of G2 Digest is shaped around a glance.

Cards you read in a glance

One screen per story with the number that matters. Swipe on if it doesn't; the dots show what's left.

Depth goes to your ears

Each card has a short glance and an optional deep dive, pre-made audio for your headphones, so your eyes stay up.

Nothing loads on the way

Editions arrive while you still have signal and stay on your phone, so the tunnel doesn't matter.

Bring your own agent

Your agent does the research. G2 Digest keeps it honest.

Connect Claude or any agent that speaks MCP and run it on your delivery days: a Claude routine, a cron job, anything that can run a prompt. It reads the card authoring guide and your brief live, so editing your brief changes tomorrow's edition with nothing else to update.

A publish only goes through after an error-free validation of that exact bundle, so what reaches your glasses always fits them.
