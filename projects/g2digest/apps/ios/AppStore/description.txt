A morning paper with exactly one reader: you. You decide what goes in it; an AI agent of your choosing writes it overnight; G2 Digest delivers it as cards to glance at and audio to listen to, already downloaded before you step outside.

THE BRIEF
On the G2 Digest website you lay out your edition like an editor: sections, the sources each one may use, how deep to go. Any agent that can publish to G2 Digest does the research and the writing. Every card is checked before it goes out.

THE EDITION
• Card by card on your iPhone, stored for the subway and the plane
• Yesterday's and last week's editions, a tap away
• A preview of how each card looks on Even Realities G2 glasses
• A sample edition to explore on first launch

THE AUDIO
Each card has a short read-aloud and many have a three-to-five-minute deep dive. Plays in the background with Lock Screen controls and adjustable speed.

ON YOUR GLASSES
With the G2 Digest plugin from Even Hub, swipe the R1 ring through today's cards and hold for the deep dive.

Getting started takes a six-digit code from the website to link the app to your edition.

SUBSCRIPTION
Installing is free; reading needs G2 Digest Pro. Start with a 7-day free trial and continue at $14.99 per year, or pay $29.99 one time instead (US pricing; the App Store displays your local price). Payment goes through your Apple Account when you confirm. The annual plan renews automatically unless cancelled no later than 24 hours before its end date, via Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
