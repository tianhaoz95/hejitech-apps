How it works

Say it the way you'd say it to your partner

No forms and no menus. Hold the mic and talk. Times, amounts and sides are understood, and anything unclear waits for a single tap.

Every way to log

Whatever is free, a hand, a wrist or your voice

Voice, on your phone

Hold the floating mic from any screen. Speech recognition and understanding both run on the iPhone, and the audio never leaves it.

One tap

Big tiles for start bottle, nursing, diaper and sleep. They change with what's happening, so "Finish bottle" appears while one is going.

Apple Watch

Speak or tap from your wrist while you're holding the baby. The phone does the processing.

Widgets, Siri and the Action Button

Log from the Lock Screen or with a press of the Action Button, and follow a running feed in a Live Activity.

Even G2 glasses and R1 ring

See the status line in your field of view, and log with a ring gesture without touching anything.

Your own AI assistant

Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants can read the log, and add to it if you allow, over MCP. Set it up.

Private by design

Your baby's data stays yours

  • Voice is processed on your iPhone. We run no speech or AI servers, and recordings from the phone and watch are never uploaded.
  • No ads and no tracking. We don't sell data, and the app has no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No account required. Use it on one phone with no sign-in, and add your partner later if you want sync.
  • Leave any time. Delete your household's data with one tap, from the app or from your account page.

Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Voice and tap logging work offline on the phone, and entries sync when you're back online.

Does my partner need their own subscription?

No. One purchase covers everyone in the household. Your partner joins with an invite code from Settings.

What if it mishears me?

When it's sure, the entry is logged and you get a few seconds to undo. When it isn't, it shows its top guesses and you pick one with a tap. It never guesses quietly.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. Android is planned. Until then, a partner on Android can follow the log through a connected AI assistant.

Does Wee Hours give medical advice?

No. Wee Hours records what happened. For questions about your baby's health, talk to your pediatrician.
