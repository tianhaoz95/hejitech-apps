Logging

How do I log by voice?

Tap the round mic button in the corner of any screen (or the Speak widget, the Control Center control or the watch complication), say what happened, and tap Done. “Started a bottle”, “dirty diaper at 3:40”, “she's done, drank about half” or, for trackers you've turned on, “tummy time for ten minutes” all work.

It logged the wrong thing. How do I fix it?

Tap Undo on the confirmation that appears. Later, you can undo it from the Timeline. Corrections are kept as new entries, so the history stays accurate.

Why did it ask me to choose?

When Wee Hours isn't sure what you meant, it doesn't guess. It shows its top options and you pick one with a tap. If you'd rather tap from the start, use the tiles on the Now screen.

Voice isn't working

Check that Wee Hours can use the microphone (iPhone Settings → Wee Hours → Microphone). The speech models ship with the app, so voice works offline. The first use after installing can take a few seconds while they load.

Can I track pumping, solids or growth?

Yes. Growth covers weight, height and head size: tap Growth on the Now tab, or say it ("she's 52 centimeters long"). Turn categories on or off in Settings. Turning off what you don't use also makes voice more accurate.

Sharing with a partner

How does my partner join?

In Settings, tap Invite a caregiver and share the code or let them scan the QR code. They install Wee Hours, sign in and enter the code. You'll both see the same log right away.

We both logged the same diaper

Wee Hours flags likely duplicates on the Now screen. Tap Merge to keep one or Keep both if they really were separate.

I started without an account. Can I add my partner now?

Yes. Choose Settings → Sign in to sync and share. Everything already on your phone is uploaded to your new household, or into your partner's if you enter their invite code.

Watch, glasses and assistants

Apple Watch

Install Wee Hours on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. The watch sends taps and recordings to your iPhone, which logs them, so keep the phone nearby.

Even Realities G2 glasses and R1 ring

Install Wee Hours from the Even Hub app and sign in with the same account. Gestures log immediately. Voice recordings are processed the next time a household phone opens Wee Hours or refreshes in the background.

Connecting Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant

Follow the AI agents guide. Manage tokens and connected assistants on your account page.

Subscription and data

What happens when the trial ends?

Tap logging, the timeline, widgets and partner sync keep working for free. Voice, the watch and glasses, reminders, assistants and CSV export need Plus.

Cancel, change plan or get a refund

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage them in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or online. Request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restoring a purchase on a new phone

Sign in with the same account. Your household's plan applies automatically, and the paywall also has Restore purchases.

Export or delete my data

Export a CSV from Settings or your account page. Delete all household data in either place removes everything for the whole household. To delete your sign-in as well, email us. See the Privacy Policy.
