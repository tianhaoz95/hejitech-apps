Wee Hours

Pair with your household

Wee Hours is active in interactive Demo Mode on your glasses. You can test all ring and temple gestures right now.

  1. Open Wee Hours on your iPhone (signed in to your household).
  2. Go to Settings → Even G2 glasses and tap Get pairing code.
  3. Type the 6 digits here. The code lasts ten minutes.

Don't have Wee Hours yet? Get it for iPhone at weehours.hejitech.workers.dev. The glasses need an account, and what you say is turned into a log entry on a household phone.
