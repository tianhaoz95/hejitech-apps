Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026.

Folio is made by HEJI TECH LLC. It stores what you and your agents put in it, so you can open it again from any agent, and nothing else.

What Folio keeps

  • Your account: your HEJI account id and email address, from Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Your artifacts: their files, every version, suggestions, comments, sharing settings and the activity feed, including which agent and model made each change.
  • Connections: the agents you authorized (OAuth grants) and hashes of any agent tokens you created. Folio never stores a token itself.
  • iPhone notifications: if you turn them on in the iPhone app, that iPhone's Apple push token and which notifications you chose. Notifications go through Apple's push service and carry the artifact's title and the agent's short message.

What Folio does not do

  • No ads, no tracking scripts, no selling or sharing data. Folio does not read your artifacts for any purpose other than showing them to the people and agents you allow.
  • Folio does not train models. The agents you connect are separate services with their own policies; they see what you let them read through Folio.

Who can see an artifact

Private artifacts: you, the people you invite, and the agents any of you connect. "Anyone with the link" artifacts can be viewed by anyone who has their link.

Where it is stored

On Supabase (database and file storage, United States) and Cloudflare (the website and its server code).

Deleting

Delete an artifact from its page, or everything at once in Settings (on the web or in the iPhone app) → Delete my Folio data. Deletion is immediate and removes your artifacts, their files and history, your memberships, tokens and notification devices. Comments you left on other people's artifacts stay there without your name.
