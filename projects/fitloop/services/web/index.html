The loop

  1. Your agent writes the plan through FitLoop's MCP connector: workouts with sets, rest and cues; meals with portions and macros; around your injuries, schedule and diet.
  2. You follow it on your phone, your wrist or your glasses: tap a set done and the rest timer starts, check off lunch, see today's progress.
  3. You say what's off, right then: "too hard", "knee hurt", "still hungry", or a note.
  4. Your agent reads it back with your check-ins and the vitals you choose to share, replies to your notes and publishes the next version.

Every data source is a switch: live heart rate, Apple Health, manual entries, progress photos, and what your agent may see.
