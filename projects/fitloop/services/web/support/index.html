How FitLoop works

1. Connect your agent

In Claude (Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector) or any MCP client, add https://fitloop.hejitech.workers.dev/mcp and sign in with the same account. Agents without sign-in support use a personal token from Settings.

Then ask: "Build me a FitLoop plan: I want to lose 4 kg by December, I have 45 minutes at lunch, a bad left knee, and I'm vegetarian on weekdays." Your agent reads FitLoop's plan guide, asks what it needs, and publishes the plan.

2. Follow it

On the iPhone, the Apple Watch, the web or the Even G2: today's workouts with sets, rest and how-to, and today's meals with portions. Tap a set done and the rest timer starts; check off a meal and say if you ate less, more or something else.

3. On the Even G2 with the R1 ring

Todayswipe through workouts and meals, tap to open, hold for progress, double-tap for the menu
Workouttap: set done (rest timer starts); on a timed set, tap to start and tap when done; swipe: next/previous exercise; hold: how-to cues; double-tap: skip, tell your agent, end
Mealtap: ate it; hold: less, more, something else, skipped, or a note

Pair the glasses with a code from the app's Glasses screen or Settings. Heart rate shows when your Apple Watch is running the workout.

4. Tell your agent, right then

"Too hard", "knee hurt", "still hungry", or anything in words: from the glasses' menu, any item in the app, or Feedback. Next time you talk to your agent it reads your notes, check-ins and the vitals you share, replies, and publishes a better version. A Sunday reminder (on by default) prompts the weekly review.

Your data, your switches

Live heart rate, Apple Health, manual entries and progress photos are each optional, and so is what your agent may see. See Privacy.

Something wrong?

Email support@waypack.app. FitLoop isn't medical advice: for pain, dizziness or chest symptoms, stop and see a professional.
