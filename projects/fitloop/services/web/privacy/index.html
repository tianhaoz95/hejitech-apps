Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026.

What FitLoop keeps

  • Your account: you sign in with Google or Apple; FitLoop receives your email address and an account id, shared with other HEJI apps that use the same sign-in.
  • Your plans: what your AI agent publishes for you, every version.
  • What you log: check-ins (sets, workouts, meals, portions, swaps), notes for your agent and your agent's replies.
  • Only the data you switch on: live heart rate during workouts (from your Apple Watch), a daily Apple Health summary (weight, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps, active energy), entries you type in, and progress photos. Each is a switch in Settings; turning one off stops it.
  • Your devices: paired glasses and agent tokens (only a hash of each token).

Your agent

FitLoop exists so an AI agent you choose (Claude, Meta's assistant, others) can coach you. An agent sees your data only after you connect it and approve it, and only what Settings → "What your agent may see" allows. Disconnect it any time in Settings. What the agent does with what it reads is governed by its provider's terms.

What FitLoop doesn't do

  • No ads, no analytics, no selling or sharing your data. Apple Health (HealthKit) data is never used for advertising, marketing or use-based data mining, and is never shared with any third party except the AI agent you explicitly connect and allow in Settings.
  • No medical advice: FitLoop shows your agent's plan and your records; talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet program.

Where it goes

Your data is stored with Supabase and processed by a Cloudflare Worker. Photos are private files only you (and, if you allow it, your agent through short-lived links) can open.

The glasses app

The FitLoop app for Even Realities G2 glasses asks for one permission, network: it talks only to fitloop.hejitech.workers.dev, our server, and fetches today's plan and sends your check-ins and notes for your agent; until you pair it, it shows a demo day and sends nothing. It stores its pairing token and its last data in the Even app on your phone. It uses no microphone, camera or location.

Who we are

FitLoop is made by HEJI TECHNOLOGY LLC. The server at fitloop.hejitech.workers.dev and the database behind it (on Supabase) are operated by us.

Deleting it

Settings → Delete my FitLoop data, in the app or on the web, removes everything above at once and disconnects your agents. Questions: info@hejitech.com.
