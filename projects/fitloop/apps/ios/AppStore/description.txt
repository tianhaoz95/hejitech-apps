You already have a coach: the AI assistant you talk to every day. FitLoop is where its program becomes reps, rest and meals you can actually follow.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Connect Claude (or another assistant) from Settings with one tap.
2. Your assistant asks about your goals, old injuries, equipment, schedule and food, then writes your week: every workout, every meal.
3. You train and eat from FitLoop. It writes the next week from what really happened.

IN THE GYM
The workout player walks you through each exercise: target reps and weight, an automatic rest timer, form cues and a looping demonstration. Swap to the alternative your coach planned, or skip and say why. Heart rate streams from your watch and the session lands in Apple Health.

AT THE TABLE
Today's meals sit next to the workout with calories and protein against your targets. Tap a meal done, or mark that you ate less or more.

TALK BACK
"Left knee twinged on lunges." "Still hungry after lunch." Notes on any set or meal go straight to your coach, who answers in Feedback and changes the plan.

THE LONG VIEW
Every version of your plan is kept. Four weeks of adherence, your streak, and weight, resting heart rate and sleep if you share them.

ON THE WRIST AND IN VIEW
The watch app shows the set, the rest countdown and your heart rate; finish a set with a tap. Even Realities G2 glasses can show the same set hands-free between reps.

WHAT YOUR COACH SEES IS UP TO YOU
Heart rate, the daily Health summary, manual entries and photos are separate switches. Health data never feeds advertising, and nothing tracks you across apps.

FitLoop shows the program your assistant writes; it is not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new training or eating plan.

MEMBERSHIP
FitLoop is free to install and runs on a FitLoop Pro membership: a 7-day free trial, then $19.99 a year, or $39.99 once for good (US prices; the App Store shows yours). Charged to your Apple Account at confirmation; the yearly membership auto-renews unless you switch it off 24 hours or more before renewal in your Apple Account settings.
