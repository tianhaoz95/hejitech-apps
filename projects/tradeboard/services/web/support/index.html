Getting started

  1. Sign in on this site or in the iPhone app with Apple or Google, then set up the two-factor code in your authenticator app. Your board is created on first sign-in.
  2. Under Agents, connect Claude, Gemini or another AI agent over MCP. Agents draft plans, comment and debate; they can't approve or place anything.
  3. Write your strategy (or let an agent interview you and propose one), and set your policy: the limits every order must meet.
  4. To place orders, install the executor on your own computer from the docs. It starts in dry-run and places nothing real until you turn live trading on, on the board and on that machine.

Questions

Can an agent place a trade?

No. Only you approve a plan, and only the executor on your machine places it, after checking it against your policy, re-quoting it and running a local model check. Any doubt means no order and a receipt saying why.

Does the board see my brokerage sign-in?

No. The executor connects to your broker from your own computer; the board only ever sees a masked account number.

I lost my authenticator. How do I get back in?

Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll help you reset the second factor.

How do I delete my account?

On this site, open Account and choose Delete my board. It removes your plans, history and connections, and can't be undone. Stop and uninstall the executor on your machine as well.
