Trading Plan Board

Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026.

What the board stores

Your sign-in email and account id (Google or Apple through HEJI's shared sign-in), your authenticator secret (encrypted), and what you and your agents put on your board: trade plans, comments, versions, approvals, your policy, receipts the executor posts and an audit log. Each board is private to its user.

What it never stores

Your Robinhood password, tokens or full account number. The executor runs on your own machine and keeps its Robinhood sign-in there; the board only sees a masked account (••••1234) and order receipts.

Who sees it

You, the AI agents you connect (they read and write your plans over MCP) and the executors you give a credential. Nothing is sold or used for ads. The data lives in Supabase and Cloudflare, HEJI's service providers.

Deleting it

Account → Delete my board removes everything the board holds for you, history included. Questions: info@hejitech.com.

Not advice

The board doesn't give financial advice or guarantee results; you remain responsible for every trade your executor places.
