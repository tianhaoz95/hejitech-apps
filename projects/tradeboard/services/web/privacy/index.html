Trading Plan Board

Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026. Trading Plan Board is made by HEJI TECHNOLOGY LLC.

What the board stores

Your sign-in email and account id (Google or Apple through HEJI's shared sign-in), your authenticator secret (encrypted), and what you and your agents put on your board: trade plans, comments, versions, approvals, your strategy and policy, receipts the executor posts and an audit log. Each board is private to its user.

What it never stores

Your Robinhood password, tokens or full account number. The executor runs on your own machine and keeps its Robinhood sign-in there; the board only sees a masked account (••••1234) and order receipts. The executor's model check runs on a model you host; plans aren't sent to us for it.

Cookies and browser storage

One sign-in cookie (HttpOnly, this site only) keeps you signed in. During sign-in, Supabase keeps a short-lived session in your browser, which the page deletes once you're in. No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

The video on the home page

The home page shows a still image. Only if you click play does it load the video from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and then Google's privacy policy applies to that playback.

Who sees it

You, the AI agents you connect (they read and write your plans over MCP, and their providers handle what they read under their own terms) and the executors you give a credential. Nothing is sold or used for ads. The data lives in Supabase and Cloudflare, HEJI's service providers. Request logs that would contain plan text are turned off.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

Your board's history is kept while your account exists, because versions, decisions and receipts are the record of what was approved and placed. Account → Delete my board removes everything the board holds for you, history included.

Children

The board is for adults who can trade in their own brokerage account; it isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on this site before the change takes effect.

Not advice

The board doesn't give financial advice or guarantee results; you remain responsible for every trade your executor places. See the Terms of Use.
