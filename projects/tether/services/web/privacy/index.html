Privacy Policy

Tether is engineered for strict security and privacy (PRD §14).

  • On-device secrets only: SSH private keys, passwords, key passphrases, and known-host SHA-256 fingerprints are stored exclusively in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage. They are never included in layout JSON, logs, or cloud sync.
  • Zero telemetry: Tether collects no analytics, no usage tracking, and no third-party crash reports.
  • Optional workspace sync: If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, only non-secret workspace metadata (tiling pane tree, bookmarks, and SSH hostnames/usernames/ports) is stored in your account and can be deleted at any time from Settings.
