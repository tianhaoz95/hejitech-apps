Caltrain, minus the guesswork. Headway knows which way you're going at this hour, which train you can still make, and when to walk out the door.

MORNING. EVENING. DONE.
Save the two legs of your commute once. Before your switch time Headway opens northbound (or southbound), after it the other way, and your watch and widgets follow along.

THE BOARD
• Every departure for your pair of stations, live from 511.org: delays, cancellations, locals, limiteds and expresses
• "Leave in 6 min" next to each train, from your walk, bike, e-scooter or drive time
• The arrival time at your stop, and when a later express beats the local leaving now
• Trains running a little behind stay on the board as "Due" instead of vanishing

ONLY THE ALERTS YOU'D WANT
Late past your threshold, cancelled, a service alert for your stretch of line, time to leave for your usual train, a nudge two stops before yours. Quiet outside the hours you pick, and done for the day once you're off the train.

RIDING
Track a train in a Live Activity and the Dynamic Island. Headway can notice you're aboard and count down the stops. Send someone a live ETA link that expires when you arrive.

LOCK SCREEN, HOME SCREEN, WRIST
Pin the next three trains to the Lock Screen, add the Next Trains widget, or glance at the watch: complications turn orange when it's time to hurry.

FAMILY ON THE LINE
Up to 8 people. Follow each other's trains until they get in, hear when someone's usual train is late. Each person chooses what to share; locations are never shared.

ALSO
Ask Siri "When's my next train in Headway". Pair Even Realities G2 glasses to see the board in your line of sight. Connect Claude or another assistant to ask about your commute.

Your location, if you allow it, is used for one walking estimate at a time and never kept. Data you save can be erased from Settings.
