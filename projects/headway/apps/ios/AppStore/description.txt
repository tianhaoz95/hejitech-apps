Headway puts your next Caltrain where you'll see it: on your iPhone, Lock Screen, watch and Even Realities G2 glasses. Pay once and everything below is yours: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads.

YOUR COMMUTE, ALREADY OPEN
• Save a morning and an evening pair of stations once. Headway opens on the right direction for the time of day, and so do your watch and widgets
• Live departures with delays, express, limited and local trains, and the arrival time at your stop
• "Leave in 6 min": when to walk out the door for each train, from the minutes you set or estimated from where you are
• Get to the station on foot, by bike, e-bike, e-scooter or car, set for each leg
• Spot when a later express gets you there sooner than the local leaving now
• Late trains stay on the board marked Due or May have left, because real trains run a few minutes behind

ALERTS THAT MATTER, AND ONLY THOSE
• Your train is running late past the minutes you pick, or is cancelled
• Caltrain service alerts for your stations and direction
• A leave-now reminder for your usual train
• A heads-up a few minutes before your stop
• Priority alerts only cover your direction inside the hours you choose; everything else stays quiet
• Once you've got off at your stop, the rest of that day's commute alerts stop

ON THE TRAIN
• Track a train with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: countdown, next stops and delays
• Headway can notice you're riding and show your next stop, arrival and stops left (optional)
• Share a live ETA link with anyone. It opens in any browser and stops when you arrive

AT A GLANCE
• Pin the next three trains to your Lock Screen
• Next trains widget for the Home Screen and Lock Screen
• A watch app with the board, and watch face complications: minutes until your train leaves and minutes until you need to go, green while you have time and orange when you need to hurry
• Runs on iPad too

FAMILY
• Make a family group of up to 8 people and invite them by email
• Follow each other's trains on your Lock Screen until they arrive
• Hear when someone's usual train is late or cancelled, or see a status like "Running late"
• Nothing is shared until each person turns it on and picks who sees it. Your location is never shared

HEADS-UP ON EVEN G2 GLASSES
Pair Even Realities G2 glasses with a 6-digit code: the next trains, when to leave and your train's progress in view, with the R1 ring to flip direction or track a train. Glasses are optional.

ASK YOUR ASSISTANT
Connect Claude or another AI assistant over MCP and ask "when's my next train home?" or "is my train late?"

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Location is optional, used for one estimate at a time and never stored. Delete everything Headway keeps about you from Settings at any time.

Sign in with Apple or Google. Caltrain times come from Caltrain's published timetable and 511.org live data. Headway is not affiliated with Caltrain.
