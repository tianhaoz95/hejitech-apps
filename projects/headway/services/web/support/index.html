How Headway works

1. Save your commute

In the Headway iPhone app (or on the web), sign in with Google and pick a morning pair (say Mountain View → San Francisco) and an evening pair. Before the switch time (noon by default) the glasses and the app open on the morning trains, after it on the evening ones.

2. Pair your glasses

Install the Headway plugin from the Even Hub in the Even app. In the Headway iPhone app open Glasses and get a 6-digit code, then type it on the plugin's page in the Even app. The glasses work without pairing too (pick stations on the glasses); pairing brings your commute and lets the glasses track a train for your iPhone's arrival alert.

3. Use the R1 ring

Swipemove through the next trains
Tapflip direction
Holdmenu: track the selected train (next stop, minutes to your stop; your iPhone alerts you before you arrive), your commute, pick any stations, alerts, the tracked train, refresh, exit
Double-tapexit Headway (from the menu, a tracked train or the station picker: back to the board)

On the board: +4 four minutes late, ok on time by the live feed, CXL cancelled. SCHED in the corner means there's no fresh live data and the times are the timetable's.

4. Alerts on your iPhone

Allow notifications in the app and choose what you want: delays past your threshold, cancellations, Caltrain service alerts for your stations, a leave-now reminder (the first train at or after your usual time, your walking time before it leaves), and an alert X minutes before arriving at your stop for the train you're tracking. Commute alerts only come on your chosen days and inside each leg's window. The Even app can mirror them to the glasses.

5. Ask an assistant

Headway has an MCP server for assistants such as Claude: add https://headway.hejitech.workers.dev/mcp as a connector and sign in, or make a personal token in Settings. Then ask "when's my next train?" or "track the 8:14 to SF".
