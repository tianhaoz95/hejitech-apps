# Privacy Policy for TinyTouch

**Last Updated: September 8, 2026**

Your privacy and your child's safety are our highest priorities. **TinyTouch does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.** The only exception is feedback a parent chooses to send from the iPhone (section 2a), which is off by default.

---

### 1. Children’s Online Privacy Protection (COPPA) & Kids Category Compliance
TinyTouch complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store Review Guidelines for the Kids Category:
* **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, locations, or any personally identifiable information.
* **No Behavioral Tracking or Advertising**: The app contains zero advertisements, tracking beacons, third-party analytics SDKs, or behavioral profiling tools.
* **Offline Operation**: TinyTouch operates entirely on-device without contacting external servers or internet endpoints, except to deliver feedback a parent sends (section 2a).

### 2. Information Collected
**None**, unless a parent sends feedback (section 2a). TinyTouch does not collect any diagnostic, telemetry, contact, financial, or usage data.

### 2a. Optional Parent Feedback (Off by Default)
On the iPhone's parent dashboard, a parent can turn on **Shake to Send Feedback** (under Help & Feedback). Only then does shaking the iPhone open a feedback form. If the parent submits it, TinyTouch sends a screenshot of the screen you shook from (you can mark it up or cover parts of it first), the note you type, and the app version, device model, iOS version and language, plus a random identifier created on the device so the app can tell you when the problem you reported is fixed. Reports go to FeedbackKit, our own feedback service, and are used only to fix the app; we never sell or share them. Nothing is sent unless you press send. Turning the setting off stops it. The Apple Watch app never sends anything.

### 3. Device Sensors and Hardware Access
TinyTouch utilizes on-device hardware capabilities exclusively for local, real-time user interaction:
* **Taptic Engine (Haptics)**: Provides immediate physical feedback when tapping or popping bubbles. No haptic telemetry is recorded.
* **Digital Crown**: Receives rotational input to animate visual elements and play musical notes. No input history is saved.
* **Audio Playback**: Plays synthesized sound effects and voice cues locally on the watch speaker. TinyTouch does NOT record audio or access the microphone.
* **WKExtendedRuntimeSession**: Utilized solely to keep the watch display active during active play sessions.

### 4. Third-Party Services
TinyTouch contains no third-party libraries, ad networks, or analytics frameworks. FeedbackKit, used for optional parent feedback, is our own feedback tool.
