# Privacy Policy for TinyTouch

**Last Updated: September 8, 2026**

Your privacy and your child's safety are our highest priorities. **TinyTouch does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

---

### 1. Children’s Online Privacy Protection (COPPA) & Kids Category Compliance
TinyTouch complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store Review Guidelines for the Kids Category:
* **No Personal Information**: We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, locations, or any personally identifiable information.
* **No Behavioral Tracking or Advertising**: The app contains zero advertisements, tracking beacons, third-party analytics SDKs, or behavioral profiling tools.
* **Offline Operation**: TinyTouch operates entirely on-device without contacting external servers or internet endpoints.

### 2. Information Collected
**None.** TinyTouch does not collect any diagnostic, telemetry, contact, financial, or usage data.

### 3. Device Sensors and Hardware Access
TinyTouch utilizes on-device hardware capabilities exclusively for local, real-time user interaction:
* **Taptic Engine (Haptics)**: Provides immediate physical feedback when tapping or popping bubbles. No haptic telemetry is recorded.
* **Digital Crown**: Receives rotational input to animate visual elements and play musical notes. No input history is saved.
* **Audio Playback**: Plays synthesized sound effects and voice cues locally on the watch speaker. TinyTouch does NOT record audio or access the microphone.
* **WKExtendedRuntimeSession**: Utilized solely to keep the watch display active during active play sessions.

### 4. Third-Party Services
TinyTouch contains no third-party libraries, ad networks, or analytics frameworks.
