## Full description (4000)

Hold your toddler without worrying about accidental taps on your watch.

When you hold a one- or two-year-old, their hands go straight for the glowing screen on your wrist. TinyTouch turns your Wear OS watch into a calm sensory playground and keeps those little fingers inside it: taps become bubbles, chimes and animal sounds instead of dismissed notifications or opened apps.

ACCIDENTAL-TOUCH SHIELD
• Full-screen play area: every touch goes to the game, and swipe-to-dismiss is turned off while your child plays.
• Parent Shield: leaving play or opening settings takes a continuous 3-second hold on the lock (5 seconds if you prefer), with a filling ring and haptic ticks. A quick toddler tap can't do it.
• Rotary input becomes part of the game: turning the crown or bezel inflates a giant bubble instead of scrolling away.
• The screen stays awake while you play.

5 LOW-STIMULATION ACTIVITIES
No fast flashing, no reward loops, no losing.
• Bubble Pop: soft bubbles with stars and hearts. Tap to pop, tap empty space for more.
• Animal Friends: Puppy, Kitty, Duck, Cow, Frog and Bunny bounce and make their sounds, with optional spoken words ("Duck! Quack quack!") for early speech.
• Sound Garden: chime bars tuned to the pentatonic scale, so every random tap sounds like a melody.
• Magic Sparkles: fingers leave trails of rainbow stardust.
• Sleepy Moon: a starry night and a music-box lullaby to wind down.

PHONE REMOTE (ANDROID)
• Switch the game on your watch from your phone with one tap.
• Set a 3, 5 or 10-minute play timer. When it ends, TinyTouch fades into Sleepy Moon instead of cutting off.
• Start the calming lullaby now, from your phone.
• See the current game, touch count and time left.
• Adjust sound effects, spoken words, haptics, low-stimulation mode and the shield hold time.
• Try every game on the phone in the sensory preview.
• Safety guide: tips for holding your toddler and locking your watch.

PRIVATE AND CHILD-SAFE
• No data collection, no accounts
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• No in-app purchases or subscriptions
• Works offline. Phone and watch sync only with each other, over your own Bluetooth connection.

TinyTouch needs a Wear OS watch (Wear OS 3 or later). The phone app is the optional remote.
