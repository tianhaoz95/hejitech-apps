🛡️ Parent Safety Blueprint: Prevent 911 / SOS Calls

Toddlers naturally grip the side of your Apple Watch. Follow these simple steps on your iPhone to prevent accidental emergency calls:

  1. Open the Watch app on your iPhone.
  2. Scroll down and tap Emergency SOS.
  3. Turn OFF "Hold Side Button to Dial".
  4. Now, holding down the side button will show sliders rather than auto-dialing 911!
